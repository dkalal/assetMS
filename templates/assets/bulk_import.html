Import Instructions
Required Columns:
  • Category: Asset category name (e.g., "Laptops", "Furniture")
  • Branch: Branch name where the asset will be located
Optional Columns:
  • Asset Name: Descriptive name for the asset
  • Serial Number: Unique serial number
  • Asset Tag: Internal asset tag/ID
  • Description: Additional details about the asset
  • Purchase Value: Purchase price (numbers only)
  • Purchase Date: Date purchased (YYYY-MM-DD format)
  • Assigned To: Email or username of assigned user
Important Notes:
  • All assets will be created under your company
  • Category and Branch must exist in the system
  • Serial numbers and asset tags must be unique
  • Invalid rows will be highlighted for review
  • You can import up to 1000 assets at once
